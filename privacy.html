Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Purpose

Actual Budget Personal is a private, non-commercial budgeting application used exclusively by its owner.

Banking data

The application uses Enable Banking to access bank account balances and transaction history from the owner's Santander Spain and Revolut Spain accounts, subject to the owner's authorization.

Storage

Budget and transaction information is stored on a privately managed Synology NAS running Actual Budget.

Data sharing

Data is processed for personal financial management. Banking connectivity involves Enable Banking and the relevant banks. The application is not offered to third-party users.

Access and control

The owner manages access to the application and can disconnect bank integrations and delete locally stored budget data.

Contact

For data protection enquiries, use the contact email registered for this application in Enable Banking.
